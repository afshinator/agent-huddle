## The finding that mattered

The ticket's claim 19 — "the most any historical row filled was 29 non-null columns" — is **false as
worded**: over the ticket's own 33-field list the maximum is 33, on row `5abda479…`; 29 is the count over
the 29 detail-fed fields. Both verifiers found it independently. Criterion 7's threshold was set from the
29-field bound, which is the one that matters, and the ticket carries the correction.

Gate 1 was worth its cost for that alone: it is the kind of claim a later ticket would have inherited.

## Why it died, both times

1. **D20's salvage never fired.** `TurnLoop` subscribes to the stream in its constructor, the coordinator
   builds it before `bootRoom()` spawns, and the real client's `register()` called `bus.reset(agent.id)`,
   which dropped every listener for that agent. `streamed` stayed empty, so `turn-loop.ts:337` was
   skipped and the timeout halted the chain. The chat pane kept printing because its listeners are added
   after the spawn — the asymmetry is why the room looked healthy. Filed as **T13**, fixed in `a5f0fce`
   with a test that subscribes *before* the spawn.
2. **900 s is not a turn budget.** First turn: 48 model waits, median **5.0 s**, p90 27.2 s, one stall of
   **354 s**, 858 s total, 45 s in tools. One provider stall cost six minutes and pushed a normal turn
   over the cap. Raised to 30 minutes (`1800000`) in the same commit.

Both fixes landed after this run, so this is the run that exposed them, not one that benefits.

## Two observations that are not tickets yet

- **A pasted multi-line pane message splits.** Two `pane.input` events fired at 22:35:18. The room
  dispatched the first; the second sat in `submitQueue` for **19 minutes** and re-entered the room as a
  new chain after the halt, with no leading `@`, as `@dev1   new queries. Then say whether…`. Pane input
  queued behind a chain should be visibly queued or refused, never resurface as an instruction nobody
  wrote.
- **The worktree reaches production credentials.** `../finance-hub/.env.local` is one directory up from
  the worktree, so "the worktree has no `.env.local`" is true and useless. dev1 found `psql` at
  `/opt/homebrew/opt/libpq/bin/psql` and ran ~15 `select`s, all reads, and its report marks claim 24
  `cannot check` because it used psql rather than the app's client. The rule is now written into both
  verifier prompts (`8980ddf`, D24): read-only selects allowed, writes and migrations not.
